Sales Analysis - Key Insights

1. Gender

- Male, Female, and Other customers are almost equally distributed.
- Male and Other customers generated more sales than Female customers.
- Male customers generated the highest profit.

2. Gender by Region

- Central → Other gender had the most buyers.
- West → Female customers had the most buyers.
- East → Female customers had the most buyers.
- North → Male customers had the most buyers.
- South → Male customers had the most buyers.

3. Region

- South generated the highest sales amount and profit.
- Central generated the lowest sales amount and profit.

4. Age

- The 30–40 age group generated the highest sales amount and profit.
- Age 35 generated the highest sales amount and profit.
- Among the buyers, Other gender customers aged 35 were the most common, followed by Female customers.

5. City

- Chennai generated the highest profit.

6. Product Category

- Electronics generated the highest profit.
- Groceries had the lowest customer satisfaction.
- Electronics had the highest number of returns.

7. Product

- Desk generated the highest profit.
- Desk belongs to the Furniture category.

8. Payment Method

- UPI was the most commonly used payment method.

9. Shipping

- Most orders took 8 days to ship.
